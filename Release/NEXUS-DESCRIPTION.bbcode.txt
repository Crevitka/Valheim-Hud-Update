[size=5][b]HUD Update[/b][/size]
A Nordic-styled replacement for the lower-left Valheim HUD: guardian power, food and health in one compact block of diamonds. Client-side, no server install required.

[b]0.1.1 — early release.[/b] Everything below works in the author's game, but the mod has not been tested with every setup yet. Bug reports and screenshots are welcome.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Guardian power diamond[/b] with white knotwork emblems for Eikthyr, The Elder and Bonemass, the power name and cooldown.
[*][b]Three food diamonds[/b] with flat, white food icons (23 drawn icon types covering vanilla foods up to the Ashlands) and a readable timer. An empty slot shows a dimmed plate.
[*][b]Vanilla-style reminder:[/b] a food icon pulses once you can eat it again, exactly like the base game.
[*][b]Two HUD styles[/b], switchable in the config while the game runs:
[list]
[*][b]Bars[/b] (default) — slanted health and stamina bars with value plates and ✚ / ⚡ icons.
[*][b]Chevron[/b] — a 2×2 food grid framed by a red health chevron with an "N HP" label; stamina stays vanilla.
[/list]
[*][b]Optional stat colouring[/b] of food icons: red for health, yellow for stamina, blue for eitr, white for balanced food. Tint, soft glow, or both. Off by default.
[*]A damage trail shows how much health you just lost.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
Install [url=https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/denikson/BepInExPack_Valheim/]BepInExPack Valheim[/url], then extract the archive into the Valheim folder so that the DLL ends up in [i]BepInEx/plugins/HUD-Update/HUD-Update.dll[/i]. The mod is a single DLL: the HUD panel and all icons are embedded.

[size=4][b]Configuration[/b][/size]
[i]BepInEx/config/crevitka.hudupdate.cfg[/i] is created on the first launch. Save it while the game runs and the HUD updates within about a second.
[list]
[*][b]General / HudStyle[/b] — Bars (default) or Chevron.
[*][b]Food / FoodIconColor[/b] — Off (default), Tint, Glow, TintAndGlow.
[*][b]Food / FoodGlowStrength[/b] — 0 to 1, default 0.7.
[/list]
A food counts as health, stamina or eitr food when that stat is at least 30 % higher than the next one; otherwise it is shown as balanced (white). Tint only affects the mod's own white icons; vanilla colour icons keep their colours and only get the glow.

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Client-side only; other players do not need it.
[*]Replaces the vanilla health panel, food icons and guardian power display (and, with Bars, the stamina bar). Mods that restyle the same HUD parts — for example [b]Auga[/b] or other full HUD overhauls — are not compatible.
[*]Item mods are fine: unknown foods get a matching icon by name (soup, pie, jam, meat…) or keep their own icon.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Known limitations[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]The HUD uses the game's own font, so it may look slightly different from the artwork.
[*]Moder, Yagluth, the Queen and Fader do not have custom emblems yet and use the game's icon.
[/list]
