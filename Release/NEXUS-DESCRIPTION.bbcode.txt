[b]HUD Update 0.2.0[/b]
Client-side Nordic HUD for Valheim by Crevitka. Requires BepInExPack Valheim.

[b]Features[/b]
Three layouts: Bars, Chevron and Yggdrasil. Seven guardian emblems, food timers and flat icons, optional food tint/glow, HUD scaling and replaceable PNG icons.
Optional ExtraSlots styling with improved food counts, faint empty ammo borders and right-aligned food/ammo rows in Bars. Supports a fourth meal supplied by a compatible mod such as OneMoreMeal. Neither optional mod is bundled.

[b]Installation[/b]
Extract the main archive into the Valheim folder. Keep only one HUD-Update.dll installation. Settings: BepInEx/config/crevitka.hudupdate.cfg. Personal icons: BepInEx/config/HUD-Update/Icons.

[b]Known limitations[/b]
No custom Eitr bar yet. Chevron stamina redesign is pending; the existing optional stamina strip is off by default. Other HUD overhaul mods may conflict. This is an early release; final package testing is still pending.
